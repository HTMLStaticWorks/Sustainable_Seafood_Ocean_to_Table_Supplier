FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is your minimum wholesale order volume?
Our minimum order volume depends on the species and delivery location. Generally, we require a 50kg minimum for domestic freight. Please submit an enquiry for specific details regarding your region.
How do you guarantee seafood freshness?
We utilize end-to-end cold chain logistics. Every shipment is temperature-monitored from the moment it leaves the water until it arrives at your kitchen door.
Can I trace exactly where my fish was caught?
Yes. Every batch comes with a unique Trace ID. Entering this ID into our traceability portal will show you the exact vessel, catch method, location, and landing date.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we offer international air freight for premium grades to select markets across Europe and Asia-Pacific. Customs documentation and export certifications are handled entirely by our team.
Are your products sustainably certified?
Absolutely. We strictly source from fisheries that are certified by leading global sustainability standards (such as MSC or ASC) or are engaged in verified Fishery Improvement Projects (FIPs).
