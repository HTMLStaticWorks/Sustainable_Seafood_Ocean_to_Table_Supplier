Our Story & Ocean Mission

BUILT AROUND THE OCEAN.
ROOTED IN UNCOMPROMISING INTEGRITY.

Tide & Trace is an ocean-to-table sustainable seafood supplier founded to transform maritime commerce. By partnering exclusively with independent day-boat fishers and certified regenerative aquaculture stewards, we deliver wild and farmed seafood of uncompromising quality directly to culinary leaders—backed by complete traceability from sea to table.

Company Story & Sourcing Methods

RESPONSIBLE SOURCING & DIRECT HARVESTING

Disrupting the Broken Chain with Direct Ocean-to-Table Accountability.

Our Story: For decades, the commercial seafood trade was clouded by opaque auction blocks, multi-tier broker markups, and destructive industrial harvesting. Catches spent days or weeks idling in transit while coastal fishers received meager margins. Tide & Trace was founded by marine conservationists and seafood purveyors to build an ethical alternative—a direct bridge between conscientious coastal harvesters and culinary professionals.

Our Sourcing Methods: We partner strictly with verified small-scale day-boats and low-impact coastal aquaculture farms in the North Atlantic, Alaska, and Pacific Northwest. We bypass industrial broker nets. Every wild catch is logged with vessel name, landing dock, and gear type, iced immediately at sea, and transported in a 24–48 hour unbroken cold chain directly to your kitchen.

Our Mission: To restore oceanic biodiversity and champion fair-wage coastal economies while providing chefs with seafood of unmatched freshness, peak culinary performance, and zero compromise on ethics.

Sustainability Practices

LOW-IMPACT HARVESTING METHODS

Selective, low-impact harvest techniques engineered to eliminate bycatch, safeguard benthic habitats, and protect marine biodiversity for generations.

Single Hook-and-Line & Trolling

Targeting fish one-by-one at optimal depths. Ensures zero ocean floor disruption and virtually 0% accidental catch of sea turtles, marine mammals, or non-target finfish.

Pot Traps with Escape Rings

Rigid baited cages for Alaskan crab and spot prawns equipped with biodegradable escape cord and sized escape rings, allowing juvenile crustacea to swim free unharmed.

Zero Bottom Trawling Covenant

Strict, non-negotiable rejection of heavy weighted benthic drag nets. We actively defend vulnerable deep-sea cold coral reefs and sensitive seagrass breeding grounds.

Marine Stewardship

OCEAN CONSERVATION & ECOLOGICAL PRACTICES

Our hands-on conservation initiatives designed to regenerate marine ecosystems, defend vulnerable habitats, and eradicate supply waste.

Marine Protected Area Sanctuaries

We strictly respect declared marine reserves, scientific nursery zones, and seasonal spawning migrations, maintaining 100% zero-harvest buffer zones around fragile reefs and living coral ecosystems.

Combating IUU Fishing & Maritime Surveillance

Coordinating with maritime authorities, naval fishery patrols, and satellite vessel tracking to eliminate illegal, unreported, and unregulated (IUU) fishing fleets from commercial supply chains.

Mangrove-Friendly Coastal Restoration

All coastal aquaculture partners preserve natural tidal flushing and actively reforest vital mangrove shoreline roots—nature's crucial nursery ground for juvenile marine life.

Ghost Gear Retrieval & Clean Oceans

We co-fund specialized technical diving missions to remove abandoned ghost nets and plastic gear from the seabed, while shipping all wholesale seafood in 100% curbside-recyclable thermal insulation.

Coastal Community Stewardship

Reinvesting a share of revenue directly into small-scale coastal fishing families and dockside infrastructure to support resilient harbor communities and fair-wage coastal livelihoods.

Zero-Waste Culinary Utilization

Encouraging whole-fish nose-to-tail culinary programs, custom portioning, and organic byproduct valorization to respect every life taken from the ocean.
