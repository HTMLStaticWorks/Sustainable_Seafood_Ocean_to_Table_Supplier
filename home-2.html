HOW OUR SEAFOOD IS SOURCED & HARVESTED

Triple-layer harvesting discipline protecting marine habitats and ensuring sushi-grade texture.

01

Selective Wild Capture

Strictly regulated day-boat fisheries targeting specific species with zero seabed disruption. Harvested via hook-and-line, trolling, and baited pot traps that prevent bycatch.

02

Responsible Eco-Aquaculture

Low-density, certified ocean pens and coastal enclosures ensuring zero preventive antibiotics, high clean water circulation, and animal welfare.

03

At-Sea Slush-Ice Chilling

Immediate slush-ice bleeding and chilling at harvest down to 0°C, locking in cellular moisture and natural ocean flavor for chef delivery.
